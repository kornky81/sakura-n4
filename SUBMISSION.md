# Oblig 3 — Sakura N4
Sakura N4 is a React/TypeScript prototype that helps students build Japanese study habits through short bilingual lessons, kanji review, reading and TTS listening. Students can set an exam target and complete a timed, ten-question shortened practice exam with saved results and explanations.
